# Research repository guidance

This repository contains a BSc materials research project. Communicate with the owner in Persian unless asked otherwise.

- Start with README.md, docs/project-overview.md, docs/milestones/phase-1.md and CHANGELOG.md.
- Store research notes, methods, references, reports and reproducible analysis in their relevant directories. Do not store chat transcripts, conversation summaries, account transfer notes or synchronization tests.
- Keep literature findings, proposed experiments and actual project measurements distinct. Do not invent experimental results or mark unread sources as fully studied.
- PDMS and conductive carbon black are the current study baseline. The third material is undecided. The former proposal is not the active plan.
- Record filler wt.% and vol.% separately. Distinguish coating bath concentration from final filler content. Include test conditions, source DOI and figure/table location for extracted values.
- Mark missing values as NR and explain limitations. Check contradictory article prose against figures before adopting numerical trends.
- Before uploading a public report, omit student IDs, private contact details and unrelated personal information. Keep publisher PDFs out unless redistribution is permitted.
- Update the relevant milestone checklist and CHANGELOG.md after real progress. Verify paths, references and remote state before reporting a successful save.
